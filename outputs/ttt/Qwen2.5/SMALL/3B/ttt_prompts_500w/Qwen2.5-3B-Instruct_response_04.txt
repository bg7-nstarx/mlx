Calculating the time required to bake 96 cookies involves several considerations, including the oven's capacity, efficiency, and the baking process itself. Let's break down the logic step-by-step:

### Oven Capacity and Efficiency

1. **Oven Capacity**: The oven's capacity is a critical factor. For this example, let's assume the oven can comfortably fit 12 cookies at a time. This means that if you need to bake 96 cookies, you would need to use the oven multiple times.

2. **Efficiency**: Oven efficiency refers to how effectively the oven cooks the cookies. A more efficient oven will cook the cookies faster and more uniformly. However, for this calculation, we'll focus on the oven's capacity and the baking process.

### Calculating the Number of Baking Sessions

1. **Initial Calculation**: If the oven can fit 12 cookies at a time, you would need to divide the total number of cookies by the oven's capacity. For 96 cookies:
   \[
   \frac{96 \text{ cookies}}{12 \text{ cookies per session}} = 8 \text{ baking sessions}
   \]
   This means you need to bake the cookies in 8 separate sessions.

2. **Session Duration**: The duration of each baking session depends on the specific cookies being baked and the oven's temperature. For simplicity, let's assume each session takes 15 minutes to bake the cookies. This is a reasonable estimate for most cookies, but it can vary based on the recipe and the oven's temperature settings.

3. **Total Baking Time**: To find the total baking time, you multiply the number of baking sessions by the duration of each session:
   \[
   8 \text{ sessions} \times 15 \text{ minutes per session} = 120 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   Therefore, the total baking time for 96 cookies, assuming an oven capacity of 12 cookies per session and a session duration of 15 minutes, is 120 minutes or 2 hours.

### Additional Considerations

1. **Preheating Time**: Each baking session starts with preheating the oven. If the oven takes 5 minutes to preheat, you need to add this time to the total baking time for each session. For 8 sessions:
   \[
   8 \text{ sessions} \times 5 \text{ minutes per session} = 40 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   Adding this to the baking time:
   \[
   120 \text{ minutes} + 40 \text{ minutes} = 160 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   So, the total time, including preheating, is 160 minutes or 2 hours and 40 minutes.

2. **Cooling Time**: After baking, the cookies need to cool. If the cooling time is 10 minutes per batch, and you have 8 batches, the total cooling time is:
   \[
   8 \text{ batches} \times 10 \text{ minutes per batch} = 80 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   Adding this to the total baking time:
   \[
   160 \text{ minutes} + 80 \text{ minutes} = 240 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   So, the total time, including baking and cooling, is 240 minutes or 4 hours.

### Conclusion

The logic for calculating the time to bake 96 cookies involves determining the number of baking sessions based on the oven's capacity, estimating the duration of each session, and accounting for preheating and cooling times. For the specific example given, the total baking time is 2 hours and 40 minutes, including preheating and cooling. Adjustments can be made based on the specific cookies and oven conditions.
