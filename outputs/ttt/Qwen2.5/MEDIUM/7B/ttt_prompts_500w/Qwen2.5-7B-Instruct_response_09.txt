Blackjack, also known as 21, is a popular card game played in casinos and online. The objective of the game is to have a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer's hand, without going over 21. Here’s a detailed explanation of the basic rules, logic, and an example of gameplay.

### Basic Rules

1. **Objective**: The goal is to have a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer's hand, without exceeding 21.

2. **Card Values**:
   - Number cards (2-10) are worth their face value.
   - Face cards (Jacks, Queens, Kings) are worth 10.
   - Aces can be worth either 1 or 11, depending on which value is more advantageous to the player.

3. **Game Setup**:
   - Players place their bets on the table.
   - The dealer deals two cards to each player and two cards to themselves, with one of the dealer's cards face down.

4. **Player Actions**:
   - Players can choose to hit (take another card), stand (keep their current hand), double down (double the bet and receive one more card), split (split two identical cards into two hands), or surrender (give up half the bet and forfeit the hand).
   - Players must hit until they have at least 12 or risk busting (exceeding 21).

5. **Dealer Actions**:
   - The dealer must hit on a soft 17 or less and stand on 17 or more.
   - A "blackjack" is an ace with a 10-value card, which pays 3:2.

6. **Winning**:
   - If the player's hand is closer to 21 than the dealer's, the player wins.
   - If the dealer's hand is closer to 21, the player loses.
   - If both the player and dealer have the same total, it's a tie (push).

### Example Gameplay

1. **Betting**:
   - Player A places a $10 bet on the table.

2. **Dealing**:
   - Dealer deals two cards to Player A: a 7 and a King (21).
   - Dealer deals two cards to themselves: a 6 and a face-down card.

3. **Player Actions**:
   - Player A decides to stand with a 21.
   - Dealer reveals their face-down card, which is a 2.

4. **Dealer Actions**:
   - Dealer must hit because their total is 8.
   - Dealer draws a 9, making their total 17.

5. **Final Comparison**:
   - Player A has 21.
   - Dealer has 17.
   - Player A wins the hand.

### Advanced Strategies

- **Counting Cards**: Players can use card counting to predict the likelihood of high or low cards remaining in the deck, which can influence their betting and playing decisions.
- **Basic Strategy**: This is a set of rules that tell players the best action to take based on their hand and the dealer's upcard. It maximizes the player's expected value.

### Conclusion

Blackjack is a game of skill and strategy, combining elements of chance and decision-making. Understanding the basic rules and strategies can help players improve their chances of winning.
