To calculate the time required to bake 96 cookies, we need to consider several factors, including the oven's capacity, baking efficiency, and the specific baking time required for the cookies. Let's break down the process step by step.

### Step 1: Determine the Oven Capacity
First, we need to know the oven's capacity. For example, let's assume the oven can hold 12 cookies at a time. This is a common capacity for many home ovens, but commercial ovens can hold more.

### Step 2: Calculate the Number of Batches
Next, we calculate the number of batches needed to bake 96 cookies. If the oven can hold 12 cookies at a time, we divide the total number of cookies by the oven capacity:

\[ \text{Number of batches} = \frac{96 \text{ cookies}}{12 \text{ cookies/batch}} = 8 \text{ batches} \]

### Step 3: Determine the Baking Time per Batch
The baking time per batch depends on the specific recipe and the type of cookies. Let's assume the baking time for each batch is 15 minutes. This is a typical baking time for many types of cookies, but it can vary based on the recipe and the oven's efficiency.

### Step 4: Calculate the Total Baking Time
Since we need to bake 8 batches and each batch takes 15 minutes, the total baking time is:

\[ \text{Total baking time} = 8 \text{ batches} \times 15 \text{ minutes/batch} = 120 \text{ minutes} \]

### Step 5: Consider Oven Efficiency and Rest Time
Oven efficiency can vary, and some ovens may require a bit more or less time to bake the cookies. Additionally, some recipes may require a rest period between batches to ensure even baking. For example, if there is a 5-minute rest period between each batch, we need to add this to our total time.

\[ \text{Rest time between batches} = 7 \times 5 \text{ minutes} = 35 \text{ minutes} \]

### Step 6: Calculate the Total Time Including Rest Periods
Adding the rest time to the total baking time gives us the complete time required:

\[ \text{Total time} = 120 \text{ minutes (baking)} + 35 \text{ minutes (rest)} = 155 \text{ minutes} \]

### Step 7: Convert to Hours and Minutes
To make the total time more understandable, we can convert 155 minutes into hours and minutes:

\[ 155 \text{ minutes} = 2 \text{ hours and } 35 \text{ minutes} \]

### Summary
To bake 96 cookies in an oven that holds 12 cookies at a time, with a baking time of 15 minutes per batch and a 5-minute rest period between batches, the total time required is 2 hours and 35 minutes. This calculation assumes standard conditions and may vary based on specific oven efficiency and recipe requirements.

### Additional Considerations
- **Preheating Time:** Ensure the oven is preheated to the correct temperature before starting the baking process.
- **Temperature Variations:** Monitor the oven temperature to ensure it remains consistent throughout the baking process.
- **Cookie Type:** Different types of cookies may require different baking times and temperatures, so always follow the specific recipe instructions.

By considering these factors, you can accurately calculate the time needed to bake a large number of cookies efficiently.
