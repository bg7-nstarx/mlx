Calculating the time required to bake 96 cookies involves considering several factors, including oven capacity, baking efficiency, and the type of cookies being baked. Here's a step-by-step breakdown of the logic used to determine the optimal baking time:

**Step 1: Determine Oven Capacity**

The first step is to determine the maximum number of cookies that can be baked in a single batch, based on the oven's capacity. This is usually expressed in terms of the number of trays or sheets that can fit in the oven. For example, if the oven has a capacity of 4 trays, each tray can hold 24 cookies (assuming a standard 24-cookie tray).

**Step 2: Calculate the Number of Batches**

To determine the number of batches required to bake 96 cookies, we divide the total number of cookies by the oven capacity. In this case, we have 96 cookies and an oven capacity of 4 trays x 24 cookies/tray = 96 cookies.

96 cookies / 96 cookies = 1 batch

This means that we only need to bake 1 batch of cookies to meet the required quantity.

**Step 3: Consider Baking Efficiency**

Baking efficiency refers to the rate at which cookies are baked in a single batch. This can vary depending on factors such as the type of cookies, oven temperature, and baking time. For example, if we're baking a type of cookie that requires a longer baking time, we may need to adjust the baking time accordingly.

Assuming a standard baking efficiency of 24 cookies per hour (based on a 24-cookie tray), we can calculate the baking time as follows:

Baking time = Total number of cookies / Baking efficiency
= 96 cookies / 24 cookies/hour
= 4 hours

However, this calculation assumes that the cookies are baked at a constant rate, which may not be the case in reality. To account for this, we may need to add a buffer time to the baking time to ensure that the cookies are baked to the required standard.

**Step 4: Consider Oven Temperature and Cookie Type**

The final step is to consider the oven temperature and the type of cookies being baked. Different types of cookies may require different baking temperatures and times. For example, if we're baking a type of cookie that requires a higher oven temperature, we may need to adjust the baking time accordingly.
