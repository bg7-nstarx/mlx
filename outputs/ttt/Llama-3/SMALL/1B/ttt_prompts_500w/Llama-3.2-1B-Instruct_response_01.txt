Subject: Request for Professional Recommendation

Dear [Manager's Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to request a professional recommendation for a new job opportunity that I have been considering. As you may know, I have been with [Company Name] for [Number] years and have consistently demonstrated my value to the organization through my hard work, dedication, and commitment to excellence.

Recently, I have been presented with an exceptional opportunity to take on new challenges and responsibilities, which aligns with my career goals and aspirations. I am excited about the prospect of joining a new team and contributing my skills and expertise to drive business success. However, I am also aware that this new opportunity may require me to leave [Company Name] and start anew.

In light of this, I am reaching out to ask if you would be willing to provide a professional recommendation on my behalf. Your endorsement would carry significant weight, and I believe it would be a valuable asset in my job search. Your recommendation would help me to stand out in a competitive job market and increase my chances of securing the role I am interested in.

I would be grateful if you could provide a recommendation that highlights my strengths, skills, and achievements. Your feedback would be invaluable in helping me to prepare for the interview process and demonstrate my value to potential employers.

If you are able to provide a recommendation, please let me know if there is any additional information you need from me. I am happy to provide any necessary documentation or information to facilitate the process.

I appreciate your time and consideration, and I look forward to hearing back from you soon.

Thank you for your support and guidance.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]

[Your Position]

[Company Name]

[Date]

Note: 

- The email is professional and polite.
- It clearly states the purpose and the request for a recommendation.
- It highlights the importance of the recommendation and the potential benefits for the job seeker.
- It provides the necessary details and information for the manager to respond.
- It expresses gratitude for the manager's time and consideration.

This draft email is a good starting point for requesting a professional recommendation. It is clear, concise, and respectful, and it provides the necessary details and information for the manager to respond.
