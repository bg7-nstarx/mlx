Calculating the time required to bake 96 cookies involves considering the oven's capacity and efficiency. Here's a step-by-step approach to determine the baking time:

1.  **Oven Capacity:** Determine the oven's capacity in terms of baking sheets or trays. For example, if the oven has a capacity of 12-16 cookies per sheet, you'll need to calculate the number of sheets required to bake 96 cookies.

2.  **Batch Size:** Divide the total number of cookies (96) by the oven's capacity to determine the batch size. In this case, let's assume the oven can handle 12 cookies per sheet.

    *   96 cookies ÷ 12 cookies per sheet = 8 sheets

3.  **Baking Time per Sheet:** Research the baking time for a single sheet of cookies. This can vary depending on the type of cookies, oven temperature, and other factors. For example, if the cookies require 10-12 minutes to bake per sheet, you can use this as a starting point.

4.  **Total Baking Time:** Multiply the number of sheets by the baking time per sheet to determine the total baking time. In this case:

    *   8 sheets × 11 minutes per sheet (avg. of 10-12 minutes) = 88 minutes

5.  **Oven Efficiency:** Consider the oven's efficiency in terms of heat retention and distribution. If the oven has a high efficiency rating, you may need to adjust the baking time downward to prevent overcooking or undercooking.

6.  **Additional Factors:** Take into account any additional factors that may affect the baking time, such as:

    *   **Temperature:** If the oven is preheated to a higher temperature, the cookies may bake faster.
    *   **Humidity:** High humidity can slow down the baking process.
    *   **Cookie Size:** Larger cookies may require longer baking times.

7.  **Final Calculation:** Based on the above factors, adjust the total baking time to ensure the cookies are baked to perfection. In this case, you may need to add 10-20 minutes to the total baking time to account for any additional factors.

By following these steps, you can accurately calculate the time required to bake 96 cookies, taking into account the oven's capacity and efficiency.
