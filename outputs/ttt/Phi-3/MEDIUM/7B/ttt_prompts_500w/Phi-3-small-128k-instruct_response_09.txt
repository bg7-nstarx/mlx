Blackjack, also known as 21, is a popular card game played between a single player and a dealer, typically at a casino. The objective of the game is to have a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer's hand, without exceeding 21. Here's a breakdown of the basic rules, logic, and an example gameplay:

Rules:
1. The game is played with one or more standard 52-card decks.
2. Each player is dealt two cards face-up, while the dealer receives one card face-up and one card face-down.
3. Numbered cards (2-10) are worth their face value, face cards (Jack, Queen, King) are worth 10, and Aces can be worth either 1 or 11, depending on the player's choice.
4. After receiving their initial two cards, players can choose to "hit" (receive another card) or "stand" (keep their current hand).
5. Once all players have completed their turns, the dealer reveals their face-down card and hits until their hand value is 17 or higher.
6. If a player's hand value exceeds 21, they "bust" and automatically lose their bet.
7. If the dealer busts, all remaining players win their bets.
8. If neither the player nor the dealer busts, the hand with a value closest to 21 wins. In case of a tie, it's a "push," and the player's bet is returned.

Logic:
The basic strategy in Blackjack is to make decisions based on the probability of improving your hand. This involves considering factors such as the dealer's face-up card, the value of your hand, and the cards remaining in the deck. By understanding these probabilities, you can make informed choices about when to hit, stand, or double down.

Example Gameplay:
Player 1 is dealt a 7 of hearts and a 9 of spades, giving them a hand value of 16. The dealer's face-up card is a 4 of diamonds.

Player 1 decides to hit, hoping to improve their hand without busting. They are dealt a 5 of clubs, bringing their hand value to 21.

Player 1 stands with a hand value of 21.

The dealer reveals their face-down card, which is a 10 of hearts. This gives the dealer a hand value of 14.

The dealer must hit, as their hand value is less than 17. They are dealt a 6 of spades, bringing their hand value to 20.

The dealer stands with a hand value of 20.

Since Player 1 has a hand value of 21, they win the round.
