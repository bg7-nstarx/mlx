Calculating the time required to bake 96 cookies involves considering several factors, including oven capacity, baking efficiency, and the arrangement of cookies on baking sheets. The goal is to ensure that all cookies are baked evenly and at the same time, without overcrowding the oven, which could lead to uneven baking or longer cooking times.

1. Oven capacity: The first step is to determine the maximum number of cookies that can be baked simultaneously in the oven. This depends on the size of the oven and the baking sheets. For example, if the oven can accommodate two baking sheets, and each sheet can hold 24 cookies, the maximum number of cookies that can be baked at once is 48.

2. Baking efficiency: The next factor to consider is the baking efficiency of the oven. This refers to how well the oven distributes heat and how quickly it can reach the desired baking temperature. An oven with good baking efficiency will bake cookies evenly and at a consistent rate, reducing the overall baking time.

3. Arrangement of cookies: To ensure even baking and optimal use of oven space, it is essential to arrange the cookies on the baking sheets properly. This means leaving enough space between the cookies to allow for proper heat circulation. A general rule of thumb is to leave at least 1 inch of space between the cookies.

4. Baking time: Once the oven is preheated to the desired temperature, the cookies can be placed in the oven. The baking time will depend on the size and thickness of the cookies, as well as the baking efficiency of the oven. As a general guideline, cookies typically take 8-12 minutes to bake.

5. Multiple batches: If the oven cannot accommodate all 96 cookies at once, multiple batches will be required. To ensure even baking, it is essential to rotate the baking sheets between the oven racks and to switch the positions of the baking sheets halfway through the baking process.

In conclusion, calculating the time required to bake 96 cookies involves considering oven capacity, baking efficiency, and the arrangement of cookies on baking sheets. By following these guidelines and ensuring even baking, it is possible to bake 96 cookies efficiently and effectively.
