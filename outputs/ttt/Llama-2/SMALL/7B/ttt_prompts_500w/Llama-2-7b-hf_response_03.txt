The current phase of the moon is waning gibbous. This phase occurs when the moon is more than 90% illuminated, but less than 100%. The significance of this phase is that it is a time when the moon is visible in the sky for a long period of time. This phase also occurs when the moon is closest to the earth, which makes it appear larger in the sky. The lunar cycle is the time it takes for the moon to go through all of its phases. The lunar cycle is about 29.5 days long.
