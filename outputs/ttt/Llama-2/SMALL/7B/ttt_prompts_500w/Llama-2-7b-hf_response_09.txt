Blackjack is a card game that is played between the player and the dealer. The objective of the game is to get a hand that is closer to 21 than the dealer's hand without going over 21. The game is played with a standard 52-card deck. The dealer deals two cards to each player and two cards to themselves. The player's cards are dealt face up, while the dealer's cards are dealt face down. The player can then choose to hit, stand, double down, or split. Hitting means taking another card, while standing means keeping the current hand. Doubling down means doubling the bet and taking one more card. Splitting means dividing the hand into two separate hands. The dealer then reveals their hand and follows a set of rules to determine if they have a winning hand. If the player's hand is closer to 21 than the dealer's hand, the player wins. If the player's hand goes over 21, they lose. If the player and the dealer have the same hand, it is a tie.
