Global warming is the increase in the average temperature of the Earth's atmosphere and oceans, and it is believed to be one of the primary contributing factors to climate change. Global warming is caused by an increase in greenhouse gases, such as carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide, in the atmosphere. These gases trap heat from the sun, which would otherwise escape into space, and cause the Earth's temperature to rise.
