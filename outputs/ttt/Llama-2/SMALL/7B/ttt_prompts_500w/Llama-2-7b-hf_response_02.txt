The UK Prime Minister in 1995 was John Major. He was the leader of the Conservative Party and served as Prime Minister from 1990 to 1997. Major was elected to Parliament in 1979 and quickly rose through the ranks of the Conservative Party. He served as Chancellor of the Exchequer from 1987 to 1990, and was elected Prime Minister in 1990 after the sudden death of Margaret Thatcher.
