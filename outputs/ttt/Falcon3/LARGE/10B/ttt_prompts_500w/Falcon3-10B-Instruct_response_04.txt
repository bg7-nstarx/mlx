To calculate the time required to bake 96 cookies, considering the oven capacity and efficiency, we need to follow a systematic approach. This involves understanding the oven's capacity, the efficiency of the baking process, and the time it takes to bake a single batch of cookies.

**Step 1: Determine Oven Capacity**

The first step is to establish the oven's capacity, which refers to the maximum number of cookies it can accommodate at one time. Let's assume the oven can hold 24 cookies per batch. This information is crucial because it dictates how many batches you'll need to bake all 96 cookies.

**Step 2: Calculate the Number of Batches**

Given the oven's capacity of 24 cookies per batch, you would need to calculate how many batches are required to bake 96 cookies. This can be done by dividing the total number of cookies by the oven's capacity:

\[ \text{Number of Batches} = \frac{96 \text{ cookies}}{24 \text{ cookies per batch}} = 4 \text{ batches} \]

**Step 3: Determine Baking Time per Batch**

Next, you need to know how long it takes to bake one batch of cookies. Let's assume each batch takes 15 minutes to bake. This time is influenced by factors such as the oven's temperature, the type of cookies, and the desired level of doneness.

**Step 4: Calculate Total Baking Time**

With the number of batches and the baking time per batch, you can calculate the total baking time. Since each batch takes 15 minutes and you need 4 batches, the total baking time would be:

\[ \text{Total Baking Time} = 4 \text{ batches} \times 15 \text{ minutes per batch} = 60 \text{ minutes} \]

**Step 5: Consider Oven Efficiency**

Oven efficiency can affect the total baking time. If the oven is not heating evenly or if the temperature fluctuates, it might take longer to bake each batch. Additionally, if you need to preheat the oven before baking, this time should be added to the total baking time.

**Step 6: Account for Cooling Time**

After baking, cookies need time to cool before they can be safely removed from the oven. Assume each batch requires 10 minutes to cool. Therefore, the total cooling time for 4 batches would be:

\[ \text{Total Cooling Time} = 4 \text{ batches} \times 10 \text{ minutes per batch} = 40 \text{ minutes} \]

**Step 7: Calculate Overall Time**

Finally, add the total baking time and the total cooling time to get the overall time required to bake all 96 cookies:

\[ \text{Overall Time} = \text{Total Baking Time} + \text{Total Cooling Time} = 60 \text{ minutes} + 40 \text{ minutes} = 100 \text{ minutes} \]

**Conclusion**

By following these steps, you can accurately calculate the time required to bake 96 cookies, taking into account the oven's capacity, the baking time per batch, and the cooling time. This method ensures that you efficiently utilize the oven and manage your time effectively. Adjustments can be made based on specific oven characteristics and cookie types to optimize the baking process.
