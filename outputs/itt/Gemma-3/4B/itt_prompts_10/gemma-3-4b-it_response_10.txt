**Overall Impression:**

The painting is a swirling, intensely emotional depiction of a night sky over a small village. It's characterized by a dynamic, almost turbulent brushwork and a strong sense of movement and feeling. It's not a literal representation of a night sky; it's a highly subjective and expressive interpretation.

**Foreground:**

*   **Cypress Tree:** Dominating the left foreground is a large, dark cypress tree. It's rendered with thick, swirling strokes of black and deep green, reaching upwards like a flame. The tree is almost sculptural in its form, with a strong verticality that draws the eye upwards.
*   **Village:** Below the cypress tree and to the right is a small village. The buildings are simplified and blocky, with warm yellow and orange lights glowing from the windows. The houses are clustered together, and a church steeple rises prominently above the other structures.
*   **Road:** A dark road leads from the bottom of the painting up towards the village, creating a sense of depth.

**Midground:**

*   **Rolling Hills:** The hills in the midground are painted with broad, undulating brushstrokes in shades of blue, green, and purple. They are not sharply defined, but rather blend and swirl together, contributing to the overall sense of movement.
*   **Mountains:** A dark, jagged mountain range appears in the distance, partially obscured by the swirling sky.

**Background – The Sky:**

*   **The Stars:** This is the most striking element of the painting. The sky is filled with numerous stars, depicted as large, glowing orbs of yellow and white. They are not uniformly placed; they are scattered and swirling in a chaotic, energetic pattern.
*   **The Moon:** A large, dominant crescent moon glows brightly in the upper right corner, radiating a warm yellow light. It's surrounded by a halo of light and swirling brushstrokes.
*   **Swirling Patterns:** The entire sky is covered in thick, dynamic brushstrokes that create a sense of movement and turbulence. The colors are predominantly deep blues, purples, and greens, with flashes of yellow and white. The brushstrokes are not blended smoothly; they are applied in short, distinct strokes, giving the sky a textured and almost three-dimensional appearance.
*   **Light and Color:** The artist uses a technique of layering colors and applying them in thick impasto (thickly applied paint) to create a luminous and vibrant effect. The light from the stars and moon seems to emanate from within the paint itself.

**Style and Technique:**

*   **Post-Impressionism:** The painting is a prime example of Post-Impressionism, characterized by its subjective interpretation of reality and expressive use of color and brushwork.
*   **Emotional Expression:** The painting is not simply a representation of a scene; it's an expression of Van Gogh's inner emotions and his intense feelings about the night.
*   **Dynamic Brushwork:** The swirling, energetic brushstrokes are a key element of the painting's style and contribute to its sense of movement and emotion.

**Overall Feeling:**

The painting evokes a sense of wonder, awe, and perhaps even a touch of melancholy. It's a powerful and deeply personal work that captures the beauty and mystery of the night sky.
